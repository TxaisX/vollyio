07 / operating guidance

What to work on next

Rules compare your assumptions with minimum economic and funnel gates. They are decision support, not guarantees.

1. Prove activationAt least half of new accounts should complete a first analysis within 24 hours.
2. Prove retentionA weekly plan needs enough repeat value to hold players for roughly eight weeks or more.
3. Prove organicUse sharing, coaches, communities, and useful content to validate the funnel before scaling spend.
4. Buy only what worksAds earn more budget only when CAC stays comfortably below contribution LTV.

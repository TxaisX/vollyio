Commercialization plan / the one viable path

Synthesis of the pose-engine benchmarks, verified competitive research, fresh recon on every live entrant, and go-to-market evidence from the four comparable apps that already won in adjacent sports.

The verdict

Ship sideout as a consumer web subscription at $14.99/mo ($99/yr) for competitive youth volleyball players and their parents, positioned as the only form coach that measures real biomechanics from one phone. Turn billing on within 30 days behind the existing D-012 seam, after clearing the license, likeness, and legal gates. Grow through shareable analyzed clips, mid-tier volleyball creators, and coach seeding. Do not build B2B club sales, native iOS, or a custom-trained model first.

05The 90-day sequence

Phase 0 · weeks 1–3 · ship gates (all blocking)

Make it legal to charge

  1. Engine license swap. The live engine string is rtm-body7-m/*; body7 weights carry non-commercial training data (AIC+MPII) and gate everything. Swap the shipped weights to a clean path before billing: MediaPipe (already a dependency, Apache-2.0) or an RTMPose-m checkpoint trained on COCO only. Run the existing measured-checkpoint evals on the swap; accept a small accuracy loss now. The retrain-on-own-volleyball-data path stays the accuracy roadmap, not the launch blocker.
  2. Device-verify the pinpoint-motion branch (current repo next step): one multi-player phone run confirms the confidence-gate fix, save fix, and tracker fix together.
  3. Resolve the standing owner items: likeness consent or hero-film swap, rotate the briefly-exposed Supabase credentials, counsel skim of /privacy and /terms, business domain + support address, hosting firewall and bot protection on auth endpoints.
Phase 1 · weeks 3–5 · monetization on

Turn the existing seam into revenue

  1. Stripe checkout + customer portal wired to profiles.plan; flip BILLING_ENABLED; free tier enforced by the existing canAnalyze reservation path.
  2. Gate the D-012 premium seam (fix detail, timestamped insights, drills) behind Pro exactly as recorded; keep the rejected dark patterns rejected.
  3. Instrument the funnel: signup, first analysis, D7 return, trial start, conversion. These numbers drive the day-90 gate.
Phase 2 · weeks 5–13 · distribution

Three channels, in evidence order

  1. Shareable analyzed clips (the product is the ad). Upgrade the share card to a video export: skeleton overlay riding the player, score, one-line fix, subtle wordmark. SwingVision's growth was users posting auto-highlights; every teen posting an analyzed rep to TikTok reaches exactly the right audience for free.
  2. Creator partnerships at 100–400K followers. Coach Donny (~218K TikTok), @volleyballmasterclass (151K IG), @coachchijo (372K IG), Coach Kiy (131K IG) already sell training to this buyer. Rev-share/affiliate, not flat sponsorships. Borrowed coach credibility is how Sportsbox and SwingVision converted skeptics.
  3. Community + coach seeding. r/volleyball (292K), the 19K-member Volleyball Coaches and Trainers Facebook group, free Pro for club coaches. Balltime rode one Facebook community to $1.2M ARR and an eight-figure Hudl exit in three years. Collect the first real coach testimonial here (none fabricated, per standing rule).

06Kill criteria and risk gates

Gate A · technical (before Phase 1 completes)

Monocular phone pose must produce defensible form scores on real devices: calibration baseline (evals/BASELINE.md) shows measured checkpoints agreeing with labeled clips, and the clean-license engine holds enough accuracy on wrist/ankle checkpoints. If measurement quality cannot beat VLM narrative in blind comparison, the moat claim is false; stop and rethink before spending on distribution.

Gate B · traction (day 90 of billing)

Thresholds: 3%+ free-to-paid conversion and 40%+ month-2 retention of paying users. Below both: fix pricing/packaging before buying creator reach. Creator spend before these numbers exist is burning the budget to learn the same lesson.

Gate C · competitive (standing)

If VolleyIQ (the only entrant claiming real biomechanics) ships multi-skill phone analysis with visible traction first, differentiate on breadth + progression + coach memory rather than measurement alone. Re-recon monthly; the category is under a year old and moving.

07What the upside looks like

Sanity math: 1% of the ~650K US serviceable market on the annual plan is ~$640K ARR; Balltime's path (community-led, $1.2M ARR, 3,800 teams) ended in an eight-figure acquisition by Hudl inside three years, and Hudl now owns volleyball stats but not volleyball form. Sportsbox exited eight figures in golf on the same "measured 3D from one phone" premise. A form-analysis product with real traction is a natural bolt-on for the exact acquirers already buying in this space. That is the realistic prize; the subscription business funds the ride.

08Explicitly not doing

  • No B2B club sales motion first (long cycles, wrong product shape; coaches are a channel, not the customer).
  • No native iOS app before live-feedback becomes the product.
  • No custom pose-model training before revenue; the clean-swap engine ships, the volleyball fine-tune is the funded roadmap.
  • No paid ads at launch; no ASO dependence (nobody in the comparable set won that way).
  • No countdown timers, fabricated projections, locked-blur teases, or fake testimonials (D-012 rejections stand).
